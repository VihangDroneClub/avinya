# New Member Onboarding Guide — Vihang Drone Club

## Welcome to Vihang

You've joined one of the most hands-on engineering clubs on campus. Whether you're here to build racing drones, work on autonomous systems, or just learn — you're in the right place.

This guide will help you get started. Read it, ask questions, and don't be afraid to break things (safely).

---

## Week 1: Orientation

### What to expect
- You'll be introduced to the club workspace and equipment
- You'll meet current members and learn about ongoing projects
- You'll get an overview of the club's history and achievements
- Safety briefing — this is non-negotiable

### What you should do
- Attend every meeting this week
- Introduce yourself to at least 3 senior members
- Ask about what projects are currently active
- Start thinking about what area interests you most

### Safety rules (read these carefully)
1. **Never fly indoors** without explicit permission and safety setup
2. **Always remove propellers** when working on a drone on the bench
3. **LiPo batteries** must be stored in fireproof bags and charged with supervision
4. **Soldering iron** — always use the stand, never leave unattended
5. **PPE** — safety glasses when cutting, drilling, or soldering
6. **Report damage** immediately — hiding broken equipment helps no one

---

## Week 2-3: Learning the Basics

### Core skills you'll need

#### Electronics
- Understanding circuits, voltage, current, resistance
- Reading schematics
- Using a multimeter
- Soldering (through-hole and SMD basics)
- Understanding ESCs, motors, and flight controllers

#### Programming
- Python is the primary language used in the club
- Basic understanding of microcontrollers (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi)
- Git basics — we use version control for all code
- Understanding of serial communication and telemetry

#### Mechanical
- Basic CAD (Fusion 360 recommended)
- 3D printing basics
- Understanding of aerodynamics (lift, drag, thrust, weight)
- Frame design and material selection

### Resources
- Ask seniors for recommended tutorials and courses
- The club workspace has reference books and printed materials
- Avinya (this system) has indexed technical guides and past project documentation

---

## Week 4: Join a Project

### How project assignment works
- Projects are led by senior members
- You'll be assigned based on your interests and the club's current needs
- You can express preference, but be flexible
- Every member contributes, even beginners

### Common project roles
- **Mechanical** — frame design, 3D printing, assembly
- **Electronics** — PCB design, wiring, power systems
- **Software** — flight control, computer vision, telemetry, ground station
- **Testing** — flight testing, data analysis, iteration
- **Documentation** — keeping records, writing reports, updating Avinya

### Your first contribution
- Don't wait to be told what to do
- Ask "what can I help with?" and mean it
- Start small — soldering connections, 3D printing parts, writing test scripts
- Every task matters, no matter how small it seems

## Club Culture & Traditions

### How we work
- **Show up** — presence matters more than skill
- **Ask questions** — no question is stupid
- **Document everything** — if it's not written down, it didn't happen
- **Share credit** — every drone is a team effort
- **Learn from crashes** — every crash teaches something

### Unwritten rules
- Clean up after yourself. The workspace is everyone's responsibility.
- If you borrow something, return it.
- If you break something, own it and help fix it.
- Seniors are here to help, not to do your work for you.
- The best learning happens when you're the one holding the soldering iron.

### Club events
- **Build sessions** — regular working meetings where everyone contributes
- **Flight days** — outdoor testing sessions (weather permitting)
- **Tech talks** — members present on topics they've learned
- **Post-competition reviews** — what went well, what didn't, what we learned

## Important Contacts

### Faculty Advisor
- Name and contact details should be posted in the workspace
- They approve budgets, competition participation, and major decisions

### Club Leadership
- President / Lead — overall coordination
- Technical Lead — project direction and technical decisions
- Treasurer — budget and finances
- Secretary — meetings and documentation

### How to reach them
- In person at the workspace (best option)
- Through the club's communication channel (WhatsApp/Telegram group)
- Through Avinya — ask "who is the current [role]?" and it will tell you if the information is indexed

## Your First Semester Goals

1. Complete safety training
2. Contribute to at least one project
3. Learn to solder properly
4. Understand the basic anatomy of a drone
5. Attend at least 80% of meetings
6. Upload at least one document or note to Avinya
