# Vihang Drone Club — History & Traditions

## Our Story

Vihang Drone Club was founded by a group of students who believed that building drones is one of the best ways to learn engineering. What started as a small group with a single kit has grown into a full-fledged club with multiple project teams, competition participation, and a culture of hands-on learning.

## Why "Vihang"

"Vihang" means bird in several Indian languages. The name reflects our aspiration to fly — not just our drones, but ourselves as engineers and innovators.

## Key Milestones

### Early Days
- The club started with a small group of enthusiasts and basic drone kits
- First builds were simple quadcopters assembled from kits
- Learning was self-directed — YouTube, forums, and trial and error
- Many crashes, many lessons

### Growth Phase
- More members joined, bringing diverse skills
- Projects became more ambitious — custom frames, autonomous flight
- First competition participation
- Established regular meeting schedule and workspace

### Current Era
- Multiple active project teams
- Structured onboarding for new members
- Budget management and institutional support
- Avinya — the permanent knowledge system

## Traditions

### The First Flight
Every new member's first successful flight is celebrated. It's a rite of passage — from someone who reads about drones to someone who flies them.

### Crash Reports
Every crash is documented. Not to shame anyone, but to learn. The best engineers are the ones who have crashed the most and learned from each one.

### Knowledge Transfer
Before seniors graduate, they are expected to:
- Document their projects and lessons learned
- Upload all relevant files to the club's knowledge base
- Train their successors on ongoing work
- Add their insights to Avinya

This tradition ensures that knowledge never leaves with a person.

### Build Nights
Late-night build sessions before competitions or deadlines. Pizza, music, and focused work. These are where the best bonding happens and the most progress is made.

### The Wall
The workspace has (or should have) a wall with photos of every drone the club has built, every competition attended, and every milestone achieved. It's a visual history of the club's journey.

## Lessons We've Learned

### Technical
- **Start simple** — the best projects start with a working prototype, not a perfect design
- **Test early, test often** — don't wait until everything is perfect to test
- **Document as you go** — you will forget what you did and why
- **Budget for crashes** — parts will break, plan for it
- **Battery safety is non-negotiable** — LiPo fires are real and dangerous

### Organizational
- **Communication beats perfection** — a mediocre plan everyone knows about beats a perfect plan no one knows about
- **Deadlines are real** — competitions don't wait
- **Everyone contributes** — no free riders
- **Celebrate small wins** — every working motor, every successful flight, every fixed bug

### For Future Members
- **Don't be intimidated** — everyone started knowing nothing
- **Be curious** — ask why, ask how, ask what if
- **Be reliable** — show up, do your part, follow through
- **Be humble** — you'll be wrong often, learn from it
- **Leave things better than you found them** — for the next batch

## What Makes Vihang Different

We don't just build drones. We build engineers.

The technical skills you learn here — electronics, programming, mechanical design, systems thinking — are valuable. But the real value is in learning how to:
- Work in a team
- Manage a project from idea to execution
- Handle failure and iterate
- Communicate technical ideas
- Teach and learn from peers

These are the skills that matter long after graduation.

## Avinya's Role

Avinya was created because too much knowledge was lost when seniors graduated. Stories, lessons, decisions, and context — all gone.

Avinya ensures that:
- New members can learn from past projects without needing to find the person who worked on them
- Technical decisions and their reasoning are preserved
- Club history and traditions are documented
- The collective wisdom of every member who has ever been part of Vihang is accessible

Avinya is not a replacement for human mentorship. It's a supplement — the member who is always here, always remembers, and always helps.
