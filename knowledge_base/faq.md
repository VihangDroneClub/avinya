# Vihang Drone Club — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Vihang Drone Club?
Vihang is the drone club of our institution. We design, build, and fly drones — from quadcopters to fixed-wing aircraft. The club is a space for students to learn aerospace engineering, electronics, programming, and teamwork through hands-on projects.

## How do I join the club?
New members are welcome. The typical process:
1. Attend an orientation session (usually at the start of each semester)
2. Express interest to any current member or faculty advisor
3. Start attending regular meetings and work sessions
4. Get assigned to a project team based on your interests and skills

No prior experience is required. We teach everything from scratch.

## What do I need to know before joining?
Nothing specific. But these skills help:
- Basic electronics (circuits, soldering)
- Programming (Python is most used in the club)
- CAD design (Fusion 360, SolidWorks)
- Physics and math fundamentals
- Willingness to learn and crash things (literally)

## What projects does the club work on?
Projects vary by semester and member interest. Common project areas:
- **Racing drones** — lightweight, fast quadcopters for competitions
- **Survey drones** — fixed-wing or VTOL for mapping and agriculture
- **Delivery drones** — payload-carrying multirotors
- **Autonomous flight** — computer vision, GPS navigation, obstacle avoidance
- **Custom builds** — whatever members are passionate about

## Where does the club meet?
Regular meetings are held in the club workspace. Check with current members for the exact location and schedule, as it may change each semester.

## Is there a membership fee?
The club may have a nominal membership fee to cover basic materials. Major components and equipment are funded through the club budget, sponsorships, and institutional support.

## Can I use club equipment for personal projects?
Club equipment is primarily for club projects. Personal projects may be allowed with approval from the project lead or faculty advisor. Always ask first.

## What competitions does the club participate in?
The club participates in various drone and aerospace competitions. Specific competitions vary by year. Past participation includes national-level drone racing, design challenges, and innovation competitions.

## Who manages the club budget?
The club has a designated treasurer and faculty advisor who manage finances. All purchases above a certain threshold require committee approval. Check the latest budget documents for current limits and processes.

## How are decisions made in the club?
Decisions are typically made through discussion in club meetings. Major decisions (budget allocation, competition participation, equipment purchases) may require a vote or faculty approval.

## What happens when seniors graduate?
This is exactly why Avinya exists. Seniors document their knowledge, upload project files, meeting notes, and lessons learned so that the club's institutional memory persists beyond graduation.

## Who built Avinya?
Avinya was built by senior members of Vihang Drone Club as a permanent knowledge repository and guide for future members. It runs locally, uses the club's indexed documents, and is designed to be the member who never graduates.

## How do I add knowledge to Avinya?
1. Upload documents through the web interface or desktop app
2. Share meeting notes, project reports, budget documents, or any relevant files
3. Add context when uploading — explain what future members should know about the document
4. The system will index the content and make it searchable

## What file types does Avinya support?
- PDF documents
- Word documents (DOCX)
- Excel spreadsheets (XLSX)
- Markdown files (MD)
- Text files (TXT)
- CSV files

## Is Avinya always available?
Avinya runs on a local server. If the server is running, it's available through the web interface on any device connected to the same network. For the most reliable access, ask the current tech lead about the server setup.
